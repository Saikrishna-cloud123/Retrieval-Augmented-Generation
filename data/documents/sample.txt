RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) is an AI framework that combines
information retrieval with text generation.

Unlike fine-tuning, RAG keeps the knowledge base separate from the model,
allowing real-time updates without retraining.

Key components include:
1. Document ingestion and chunking
2. Embedding generation
3. Vector storage and retrieval
4. LLM-based answer generation

RAG systems are widely used in enterprise search, customer support,
and knowledge management applications.
